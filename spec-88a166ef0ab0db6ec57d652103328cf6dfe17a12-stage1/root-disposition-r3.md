## Sustained changes

Root sustains referee E's G1--G3 as P1 specification and planning-closure
risks. They are not classified as current-production emergencies because this
Stage 1 programme is not operative and has no authorized production
implementation. This administrative classification does not alter the
referee's original report or findings.

1. **Six-file activation authority.** The repository root `README.md` must join
   the five blueprint files in the exact successor activation set. It must stop
   presenting Olver-first scheduling as unqualified current global authority
   and instead make the DLMF-priority rule, pending/effective lifecycle, and
   parallel Olver-track boundary consistent across all six exact-bound files.
   A repository-wide check must show that every surviving first-programme or
   MVP statement is either the effective DLMF rule or explicitly local to the
   parallel Olver track.

2. **Canonical payload identity and alias reconciliation.** The plan must
   define one payload-owning retrieval document for each DLMF content family
   after redirect normalization. Fragments, equation or subsection permalinks,
   format variants, and query/customization variants must be reconciled as
   locators or representations unless exact-byte audit proves a distinct
   payload. Alias reconciliation must precede `source_appearance_id` creation,
   while genuinely repeated appearances within one canonical payload remain
   distinct occurrences. Required fixtures must reject duplicate appearances
   minted from `/10.61`, `/10.61.i`, and `/10.61.E1` for the same printed
   formula.

3. **Separate correction and evolution relations.** Same-snapshot local
   correction/atomicization supersession and cross-snapshot evolution must be
   separate typed relations. A same-snapshot correction remains local to one
   source appearance and may change that snapshot's effective atomization;
   it may not merge distinct printed appearances. Cross-snapshot evolution may
   record unchanged, modified, split, merged, added, or removed mappings, but
   it must never deactivate or rewrite the predecessor snapshot's effective
   target set. Activity is relative to the selected snapshot and that
   snapshot's reviewed correction state.

   In addition, every audit manifest and global-closure record must pin an
   immutable correction view. A later same-snapshot correction therefore
   creates a new view/successor authority rather than mutating the denominator
   reconstructed by an earlier closed manifest. Fixtures must prove both that
   a same-snapshot merge across distinct appearances is rejected and that later
   local correction or snapshot evolution leaves every previously closed
   denominator reproducible byte-for-byte and set-for-set.
