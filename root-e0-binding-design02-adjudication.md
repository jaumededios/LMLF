## Consolidated architectural disposition

All seven findings remain open pending successor design and fresh review.
The following choices authorize only natural-language successor writing:

1. D-G1: use a closed `reviewer_verdict` discriminator for pre-signature-gate
   versus batch-closure subjects, with disjoint fields, subject kinds, and
   permitted consumers. Preserve the accepted authority-edge grammar.
2. D-G2: provide one finite wire appendix with exact headers, kind/codec
   dispatch, recursive fields, enums, references, null rules, ordering, and
   uniqueness. Card and declaration source-batch endpoints are independently
   pending/supplied. Intrinsic frozen metadata on a supplied artifact does not
   turn that row into a frozen binding. Do not create a general schema engine.
3. D-G3: represent NLP applicability and review gates per role. Exact proof/NR
   coverage must agree with each frozen card. Common proof content may serve
   several roles; same-claim mixed paths must be tested. Structural review
   remains independent. Use all five protocol perspective tokens.
4. C-G1: bind a finite mathematical support manifest before proof review. It
   fixes used mathematical dependencies, hypotheses, conventions, prerequisites,
   and pinned interfaces without introducing proposed Lean signatures. The
   later correspondence cannot add or replace mathematical dependencies or
   pins. Tactics and import plumbing are not mathematical premises. Preserve
   acyclicity between support data, cards, and subjects.
5. C-G2: explicitly bind exact classification-successor bytes and their exact
   immutable v3 predecessor. Schema syntax is not classification authority.
6. C-G3: add bounded earlier retrieval-evidence records reached through the
   manifest-bound retrieval CSV. Specify locator, method, redirects, observed
   version evidence, availability/access, and rights provenance. Truthfully
   unavailable or expired evidence is distinct from malformed evidence and
   cannot support synthetic readiness. No network, rights, or authority
   automation is authorized.
7. C-G4: represent all protocol-permitted NLP exemptions: definitions,
   audit-only targets, and transparent non-novel pinned-library reuse, each
   with its proper exact applicability subject and reason. This does not let
   support/audit-only work substitute for a source target or source coverage.

The successor must retain the exact finite core-manifest boundary, raw-byte
identity, document-qualified spans, standalone requested validation levels,
proof-before-signature order, and manual truth/authority boundaries. Root has
assigned this writing to Sol. New binding code still waits for reviewed exact
successor bytes.
