§4.35 Identities🔗

DLMF §4.35 gives the addition, product, multiple-angle, and modulus identities for the complex hyperbolic functions. Each display below links directly to its formula-specific DLMF entry. Quotient identities include the nonvanishing hypotheses needed for ordinary complex division.

The local complexCoth definition is a totalized quotient: at a zero of Complex.sinh, Lean's division returns zero rather than an analytic pole. Accordingly, the coth formulas below explicitly assume the relevant sinh values are nonzero; outside that domain the definition is only a totalized convenience, not a claim that coth is analytic there.

Shared vocabulary: complex coth
def complexCoth (z : ℂ) : ℂ := Complex.cosh z / Complex.sinh z
DLMF 4.35.E1 ↗

Addition of sinh arguments expands both the sum and difference with their signed cross terms.

DLMF 4.35.E1
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_1 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.sinh (u + v) = Complex.sinh u * Complex.cosh v + Complex.cosh u * Complex.sinh v ∧ Complex.sinh (u - v) = Complex.sinh u * Complex.cosh v - Complex.cosh u * Complex.sinh v
DLMF 4.35.E2 ↗

Addition of cosh arguments gives the corresponding even-function sum and difference forms.

DLMF 4.35.E2
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_2 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.cosh (u + v) = Complex.cosh u * Complex.cosh v + Complex.sinh u * Complex.sinh v ∧ Complex.cosh (u - v) = Complex.cosh u * Complex.cosh v - Complex.sinh u * Complex.sinh v
DLMF 4.35.E3 ↗

The tanh addition and subtraction quotients require nonzero cosh factors and displayed denominators.

DLMF 4.35.E3
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_3 (u v : ℂ) (hu : Complex.cosh u ≠ 0) (hv : Complex.cosh v ≠ 0) (hplus : 1 + Complex.tanh u * Complex.tanh v ≠ 0) (hminus : 1 - Complex.tanh u * Complex.tanh v ≠ 0) : Complex.tanh (u + v) = (Complex.tanh u + Complex.tanh v) / (1 + Complex.tanh u * Complex.tanh v) ∧ Complex.tanh (u - v) = (Complex.tanh u - Complex.tanh v) / (1 - Complex.tanh u * Complex.tanh v)
DLMF 4.35.E4 ↗

The coth addition and subtraction quotients are stated on the pole-free domain of the reciprocal vocabulary.

DLMF 4.35.E4
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_4 (u v : ℂ) (hu : Complex.sinh u ≠ 0) (hv : Complex.sinh v ≠ 0) (hplus : complexCoth u + complexCoth v ≠ 0) (hminus : complexCoth u - complexCoth v ≠ 0) : complexCoth (u + v) = (complexCoth u * complexCoth v + 1) / (complexCoth u + complexCoth v) ∧ complexCoth (u - v) = (-complexCoth u * complexCoth v + 1) / (complexCoth u - complexCoth v)
DLMF 4.35.E5 ↗

The sum of two sinh values is the product of sinh and cosh at the half-sum and half-difference.

DLMF 4.35.E5
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_5 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.sinh u + Complex.sinh v = 2 * Complex.sinh ((u + v) / 2) * Complex.cosh ((u - v) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E6 ↗

The difference of two sinh values is the complementary cosh–sinh product at half arguments.

DLMF 4.35.E6
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_6 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.sinh u - Complex.sinh v = 2 * Complex.cosh ((u + v) / 2) * Complex.sinh ((u - v) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E7 ↗

The sum of two cosh values factors into cosh at the half-sum and half-difference.

DLMF 4.35.E7
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_7 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.cosh u + Complex.cosh v = 2 * Complex.cosh ((u + v) / 2) * Complex.cosh ((u - v) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E8 ↗

The difference of two cosh values factors into the corresponding sinh product.

DLMF 4.35.E8
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_8 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.cosh u - Complex.cosh v = 2 * Complex.sinh ((u + v) / 2) * Complex.sinh ((u - v) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E9 ↗

The tanh sum and difference are written as sinh quotients under the nonzero cosh hypotheses.

DLMF 4.35.E9
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_9 (u v : ℂ) (hu : Complex.cosh u ≠ 0) (hv : Complex.cosh v ≠ 0) : Complex.tanh u + Complex.tanh v = Complex.sinh (u + v) / (Complex.cosh u * Complex.cosh v) ∧ Complex.tanh u - Complex.tanh v = Complex.sinh (u - v) / (Complex.cosh u * Complex.cosh v)
DLMF 4.35.E10 ↗

The coth sum and difference are written as sinh quotients under the nonzero sinh hypotheses.

DLMF 4.35.E10
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_10 (u v : ℂ) (hu : Complex.sinh u ≠ 0) (hv : Complex.sinh v ≠ 0) : complexCoth u + complexCoth v = Complex.sinh (v + u) / (Complex.sinh u * Complex.sinh v) ∧ complexCoth u - complexCoth v = Complex.sinh (v - u) / (Complex.sinh u * Complex.sinh v)
DLMF 4.35.E11 ↗

The fundamental hyperbolic Pythagorean identity relates the squared cosh and sinh values.

DLMF 4.35.E11
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_11 (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh z ^ 2 - Complex.sinh z ^ 2 = 1
DLMF 4.35.E12 ↗

Taking the reciprocal square of cosh yields the stated identity involving tanh.

DLMF 4.35.E12
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_12 (z : ℂ) (h : Complex.cosh z ≠ 0) : (1 / Complex.cosh z) ^ 2 = 1 - Complex.tanh z ^ 2
DLMF 4.35.E13 ↗

Taking the reciprocal square of sinh yields the corresponding identity involving coth.

DLMF 4.35.E13
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_13 (z : ℂ) (h : Complex.sinh z ≠ 0) : (1 / Complex.sinh z) ^ 2 = complexCoth z ^ 2 - 1
DLMF 4.35.E14 ↗

The product of two sinh values is recovered by subtracting the two cosh addition forms.

DLMF 4.35.E14
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_14 (u v : ℂ) : 2 * Complex.sinh u * Complex.sinh v = Complex.cosh (u + v) - Complex.cosh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E15 ↗

The product of two cosh values is recovered by adding the two cosh addition forms.

DLMF 4.35.E15
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_15 (u v : ℂ) : 2 * Complex.cosh u * Complex.cosh v = Complex.cosh (u + v) + Complex.cosh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E16 ↗

The mixed sinh–cosh product is recovered by adding the two sinh addition forms.

DLMF 4.35.E16
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_16 (u v : ℂ) : 2 * Complex.sinh u * Complex.cosh v = Complex.sinh (u + v) + Complex.sinh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E17 ↗

The difference of squared sinh values factors into sinh at the sum and difference arguments.

DLMF 4.35.E17
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_17 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.sinh u ^ 2 - Complex.sinh v ^ 2 = Complex.sinh (u + v) * Complex.sinh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E18 ↗

The difference of squared cosh values has the same sum-and-difference factorization.

DLMF 4.35.E18
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_18 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.cosh u ^ 2 - Complex.cosh v ^ 2 = Complex.sinh (u + v) * Complex.sinh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E19 ↗

The mixed sum of squared sinh and cosh values factors into a product of cosh terms.

DLMF 4.35.E19
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_19 (u v : ℂ) : Complex.sinh u ^ 2 + Complex.cosh v ^ 2 = Complex.cosh (u + v) * Complex.cosh (u - v)
DLMF 4.35.E20 ↗

For nonnegative real x, the principal square-root branch gives the half-angle sinh identity.

DLMF 4.35.E20
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_20 {x : ℝ} (hx : 0 ≤ x) : Complex.sinh ((x : ℂ) / 2) = Complex.sqrt ((Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) - 1) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E21 ↗

For nonnegative real x, the principal square-root branch gives the half-angle cosh identity.

DLMF 4.35.E21
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_21 {x : ℝ} (hx : 0 ≤ x) : Complex.cosh ((x : ℂ) / 2) = Complex.sqrt ((Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) + 1) / 2)
DLMF 4.35.E22 ↗

For positive real x, the principal half-angle square-root quotient agrees with both rational forms.

DLMF 4.35.E22
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_22 {x : ℝ} (hx : 0 < x) : Complex.tanh ((x : ℂ) / 2) = Complex.sqrt ((Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) - 1) / (Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) + 1)) ∧ Complex.tanh ((x : ℂ) / 2) = (Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) - 1) / Complex.sinh (x : ℂ) ∧ Complex.tanh ((x : ℂ) / 2) = Complex.sinh (x : ℂ) / (Complex.cosh (x : ℂ) + 1)
DLMF 4.35.E23 ↗

Sinh is odd, giving the sign reversal under negation.

DLMF 4.35.E23
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_23 (z : ℂ) : Complex.sinh (-z) = -Complex.sinh z
DLMF 4.35.E24 ↗

Cosh is even, so negating the argument leaves it unchanged.

DLMF 4.35.E24
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_24 (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh (-z) = Complex.cosh z
DLMF 4.35.E25 ↗

Tanh is odd, with the same totalized complex quotient convention as Mathlib.

DLMF 4.35.E25
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_25 (z : ℂ) : Complex.tanh (-z) = -Complex.tanh z
DLMF 4.35.E26 ↗

The double-angle sinh identity includes its tanh quotient form under the displayed denominator condition.

DLMF 4.35.E26
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_26 (z : ℂ) (h : 1 - Complex.tanh z ^ 2 ≠ 0) : Complex.sinh (2 * z) = 2 * Complex.sinh z * Complex.cosh z ∧ Complex.sinh (2 * z) = 2 * Complex.tanh z / (1 - Complex.tanh z ^ 2)
DLMF 4.35.E27 ↗

The three equivalent double-angle cosh forms are valid throughout the complex domain.

DLMF 4.35.E27
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_27 (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh (2 * z) = 2 * Complex.cosh z ^ 2 - 1 ∧ Complex.cosh (2 * z) = 2 * Complex.sinh z ^ 2 + 1 ∧ Complex.cosh (2 * z) = Complex.cosh z ^ 2 + Complex.sinh z ^ 2
DLMF 4.35.E28 ↗

The double-angle tanh quotient requires the displayed nonzero denominator.

DLMF 4.35.E28
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_28 (z : ℂ) (h : 1 + Complex.tanh z ^ 2 ≠ 0) : Complex.tanh (2 * z) = 2 * Complex.tanh z / (1 + Complex.tanh z ^ 2)
DLMF 4.35.E29 ↗

The triple-angle sinh polynomial is expressed in powers of sinh.

DLMF 4.35.E29
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_29 (z : ℂ) : Complex.sinh (3 * z) = 3 * Complex.sinh z + 4 * Complex.sinh z ^ 3
DLMF 4.35.E30 ↗

The triple-angle cosh polynomial is expressed in powers of cosh.

DLMF 4.35.E30
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_30 (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh (3 * z) = -3 * Complex.cosh z + 4 * Complex.cosh z ^ 3
DLMF 4.35.E31 ↗

The quadruple-angle sinh expansion separates the two cubic mixed terms.

DLMF 4.35.E31
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_31 (z : ℂ) : Complex.sinh (4 * z) = 4 * Complex.sinh z ^ 3 * Complex.cosh z + 4 * Complex.cosh z ^ 3 * Complex.sinh z
DLMF 4.35.E32 ↗

The quadruple-angle cosh expansion is the symmetric fourth-degree expression.

DLMF 4.35.E32
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_32 (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh (4 * z) = Complex.cosh z ^ 4 + 6 * Complex.sinh z ^ 2 * Complex.cosh z ^ 2 + Complex.sinh z ^ 4
DLMF 4.35.E33 ↗

The integer-multiple formula identifies powers of cosh z plus or minus sinh z.

DLMF 4.35.E33
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_33 (n : ℤ) (z : ℂ) : Complex.cosh ((n : ℂ) * z) + Complex.sinh ((n : ℂ) * z) = (Complex.cosh z + Complex.sinh z) ^ n ∧ Complex.cosh ((n : ℂ) * z) - Complex.sinh ((n : ℂ) * z) = (Complex.cosh z - Complex.sinh z) ^ n
DLMF 4.35.E34 ↗

The sinh value on a complex argument is decomposed into its real and imaginary coordinates.

DLMF 4.35.E34
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_34 (x y : ℝ) : Complex.sinh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y) = Real.sinh x * Real.cos y + Complex.I * (Real.cosh x * Real.sin y)
DLMF 4.35.E35 ↗

The cosh value on a complex argument is decomposed into its real and imaginary coordinates.

DLMF 4.35.E35
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_35 (x y : ℝ) : Complex.cosh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y) = Real.cosh x * Real.cos y + Complex.I * (Real.sinh x * Real.sin y)
DLMF 4.35.E36 ↗

The tanh addition quotient at a doubled argument requires a nonzero cosh denominator.

DLMF 4.35.E36
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_36 (x y : ℝ) (h : Real.cosh (2 * x) + Real.cos (2 * y) ≠ 0) : Complex.tanh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y) = (Real.sinh (2 * x) + Complex.I * Real.sin (2 * y)) / (Real.cosh (2 * x) + Real.cos (2 * y))
DLMF 4.35.E37 ↗

The coth subtraction quotient at a doubled argument requires a nonzero sinh denominator.

DLMF 4.35.E37
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_37 (x y : ℝ) (h : Real.cosh (2 * x) - Real.cos (2 * y) ≠ 0) : complexCoth ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y) = (Real.sinh (2 * x) - Complex.I * Real.sin (2 * y)) / (Real.cosh (2 * x) - Real.cos (2 * y))
DLMF 4.35.E38 ↗

The modulus of sinh is the square root of the sum of the squared real-coordinate terms.

DLMF 4.35.E38
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_38 (x y : ℝ) : ‖Complex.sinh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y)‖ = Real.sqrt (Real.sinh x ^ 2 + Real.sin y ^ 2)
DLMF 4.35.E39 ↗

The modulus of cosh is the square root of the sum of the squared real-coordinate terms.

DLMF 4.35.E39
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_39 (x y : ℝ) : ‖Complex.cosh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y)‖ = Real.sqrt (Real.sinh x ^ 2 + Real.cos y ^ 2)
DLMF 4.35.E40 ↗

The modulus of tanh is the square root of the quotient of the corresponding double-angle quantities, under the nonzero cosh condition.

DLMF 4.35.E40
theorem declaration uses `sorry`dlmf_4_35_40 (x y : ℝ) (h : Real.cosh (2 * x) + Real.cos (2 * y) ≠ 0) : ‖Complex.tanh ((x : ℂ) + Complex.I * y)‖ = Real.sqrt ((Real.cosh (2 * x) - Real.cos (2 * y)) / (Real.cosh (2 * x) + Real.cos (2 * y)))
